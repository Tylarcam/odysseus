60-second intro

Script — use now

“I come from building data and AI products where the hardest part isn’t just the model — it’s turning messy, fragmented signals into something customers trust and use. I’ve led end-to-end pipelines from ingestion and identity through to the product experiences on top, balancing fast iteration with data quality, privacy, and reliability. What excites me about Smoosh AI is you’re tackling that exact problem in fandom: helping orgs understand and serve fans by unifying data that’s usually stuck in silos. As CTO I’d focus on three things: architecture simple enough to scale, a delivery rhythm that ships meaningful improvements regularly, and communication so engineering through GTM stay aligned on what we’re solving and why.”

04

Smart questions for Janice

Pick 4–6. Lead with success definition.

  • If we’re wildly successful in 24 months, what is Smoosh AI known for?
  • What’s the single most important product outcome in the next 6–12 months?
  • Which data sources are most critical today — and which are most painful?
  • Where’s the bottleneck: data reliability, AI performance, product velocity, or GTM fit?
  • How do customers describe the aha moment when Smoosh is really working?
  • What are non-negotiables — privacy, latency, explainability?
  • What does success look like for the CTO in the first 90 days?
  • How do you like to work with a CTO weekly — decisions, updates, feedback?
  • Any partnerships, investors, or customers that impose hard technical requirements?
  • If I joined, what’s the first technical or product decision you’d want help with?

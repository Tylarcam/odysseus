## 4. Your stories (filled + interview-ready)

> **Bridge language:** Ethos Lab isn’t CPG — translate on the call: *pilot site → paid rollout*, *students/parents → users*, *schools → accounts/seats*, *curriculum onboarding → 8–12 week implementation*.

### A. Customer expansion → maps to 5–10x existing accounts

**Raw:** Ethos Lab EdTech; ground zero (one physical lab) → district curriculum for 80+ Vancouver elementary schools. Built BC-standards curriculum, piloted 3 schools + 1 district assembly (4 workshops, VR/AR/GenAI). Result: $1M Unity/Meta grant; just opened owned lab space (was renting).

**Improved (say this):**
- **Starting state:** One location, one use case — kids visit the lab after school. No scalable product.
- **Expansion play:** Turned internal programming into a **repeatable in-school product** aligned to BC learning standards — same motion as taking one MorningAI use case and rolling it across brands/divisions.
- **Pilot → proof:** 3 elementary schools + 1 assembly day (4 grade-level workshops: VR, AR, Stable Diffusion).
- **Results:** $1M Unity/Meta grant to scale; **inbound expansion** from Surrey (104 schools, outside Vancouver’s 89); Ethos **bought their lab** last week (rent → own) — expansion funded by program success.
- **At MorningAI:** Customer intake on bottlenecks + outcome alignment → bridge customer voice to eng/product → expansion plan per account (more users, formats, brands) before chasing new logos.

### B. Nailing delivery → maps to “nail the work”

**Raw:** ASP enrollment surged; proposed in-school product as onboarding funnel into spring/summer programs.

**Improved (say this):**
- **Problem:** Delivery couldn’t scale — physical lab had a **capacity ceiling** while demand spiked (like usage growing faster than onboarding capacity).
- **Move:** Built a **lighter-weight in-school onboarding product** — teachers run curriculum in-class → students funnel into ASP/spring/summer (structured handoff, not ad hoc).
- **Result:** Cut time-to-enrollment by **3 weeks**; spring/summer seats confirmed **before** deadline.
- **Ops stack:** **Notion CRM** (schools, families, pipeline stage) + **internal spreadsheet** (enrollment tracking, capacity) + **teacher onboarding checklist** (repeatable handoff from pilot → live classroom).
- **MorningAI parallel:** When brands adopt fast, you need a **defined onboarding path** (their 8–12 weeks) so usage compounds instead of stalling at DNA setup or approvals.

### C. Full-stack GTM solo → maps to “drive most of GTM”

**Raw:** Owned proposal → pilot → close → onboard → expand at Ethos Lab.

**Improved (say this):**
- **Loop:** Wrote in-school proposal → sent to interested schools → **ran pilots in person** (3 schools, 1-hr workshops) → closed → onboarded teachers with curriculum + lab access → **+50 families** enrolled.
- **Expand:** Success drove **Surrey district inbound** (104 schools) without outbound.
- **Ops:** **Notion CRM** + enrollment spreadsheet + teacher checklists — single source of truth from pilot → onboard → expand. No sales team, no CS layer. Founder-style GTM + ops.
- **AI SaaS backup:** Buildspace (studio vertical + 16K-view launch), Kontigo (pooled financial model → validated scale), Product Spec Tracer (shipped to Chrome fast).
